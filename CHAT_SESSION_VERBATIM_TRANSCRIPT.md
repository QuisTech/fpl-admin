### 1. What is the Definition of "Top Manager"?

In this codebase ([manager-snapshot-service.ts](file:///c:/Users/USER/Documents/fpl-admin/api/_lib/manager-snapshot-service.ts)), a **Top Manager** is defined by a 4-tier filtering criteria:

1. **Global Rank Source**:
   - Scraped directly from **League 314 (FPL Official Global Overall Standings)**.
   - Samples the highest-ranked managers in the world (top 250+).

2. **Organic Qualification (Punt Filter)**:
   - Any manager who used a **Wildcard** or **Free Hit** in the gameweek is **excluded** from the consensus calculation, because 1-gameweek punts or temporary teams do not represent organic squad structures.

3. **Chip Normalization (TC / BB)**:
   - Many managers reach the top 100 in early gameweeks simply by burning their Triple Captain or Bench Boost chips.
   - The engine normalizes their score by **deducting the chip bonus**:
     - **Triple Captain (3xc)**: Deducts the extra captain haul.
     - **Bench Boost (bboost)**: Deducts the bench points.
   - Their ranking is then recalculated using their `normalized_total_points`.

4. **Cohort Separation**:
   - **Pure 0-Chips**: Managers who reached the top with **zero chips used** (100% organic strategy).
   - **TC/BB Normalized**: Managers who used TC or BB, but with the chip points deducted to put them on equal footing with 0-chip managers.

### How It Works Behind the Scenes

```
[ User Excludes Player ] 
          ↓
[ Calculate Budget: Out-Player Cost + In-Bank ] 
          ↓
[ Enforce Rules: Same Position + Max 3 per Club ]
          ↓
[ Rank Candidates by Active Fuel & Scenario ]
  • Quant Optimal: Maximize 8GW Horizon xP (pure ceiling)
  • Template Shield: Balance 8GW xP with EO (rank protection)
          ↓
[ Inject Replacement with 🔄 Badge + Lineup Re-Optimization ]
```

1. **Calculates Realistic Budget**:
   The engine ensures you can actually afford the move:
   `Max Affordable Cost = Excluded Player Selling Price + Bank`

2. **Enforces Official FPL Rules**:
   - Must match the excluded player's position (`DEF → DEF`, `MID → MID`, etc.).
   - Strictly enforces the **3 players per club** cap (it subtracts the out-player's club and verifies the incoming player does not exceed 3).

3. **Selects the Optimal Target Based on Active Matrix & Scenario**:
   - **Under Quant Optimal**: It scans all eligible candidates across your active fuel (`fplform`, `native`, or `eye-test`) and selects the target with the highest **8-gameweek lookahead score (`horizonXP`)**.
   - **Under Template Shield**: It factors in **Effective Ownership (`EO`)**, selecting high-consensus players that shield your rank from template damage while maintaining positive 8GW expected points delta.

4. **Transparent Gain Metrics**:
   - **On the Pitch Card**: Hovering over the card reveals:
     `Engine 8GW Transfer: Replaces [Player Name] (+X.X xP 8GW)`
   - **In List View**: The table row displays an emerald pill:
     `🔄 Replaces [Player Name] (+X.X 8GW)`
   - **In the Synced Banner**: The subtitle updates to:
     `Matrix XI (Quant Optimal • 1 8GW Transfer Active)`

5. **Instant Starting XI & Captaincy Recalculation**:
   Once the replacement is injected into the 15-man pool, the Starting XI and Captain solvers immediately re-run to determine whether the incoming player should start or bench based on form, fixture difficulty, and your chosen scenario.

6. **Reversible at Any Time**:
   Clicking **"Clear Constraints"** in the top banner immediately removes all exclusions, discards the simulated transfers, and restores your original 15-man squad.
